Questions we get asked

Yes. Send us your current cPanel login and we migrate the files, databases and email for free, usually within one working day.

Hostia Ultra is for people running more than one site, or one site that has started to matter. Every account gets its own cPanel, a LiteSpeed web server with server-side caching, and SSL certificates that renew without you thinking about them.

Choose the region when you order. Dubai is served from Equinix DX1, Virginia from Ashburn, Singapore from SG1. Moving between regions later is a support ticket, not a migration project.

What is included

One-click installers for WordPress, Laravel and 400 other applications. Free migration from your current host. Daily malware scanning, email spam filtering, and a 30-day money-back period on first orders.

What is not

A domain name. Add one at the checkout, or point an existing domain at the nameservers we give you. Dedicated IPs and extra storage are available as addons once the account is active.

Can I upgrade to Infinity later?

Yes, from the client area. The difference is prorated and the account is resized in place.

Does unlimited really mean unlimited?

Unlimited websites and mailboxes within the 50 GB of disk and a fair-use CPU allowance of 100% of one core.
