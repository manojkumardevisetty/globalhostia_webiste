Questions

What does Ultra renew at?

The same price you pay now: $5.99/mo or $71.88/yr. We email a reminder 14 days before renewal and you can cancel from the dashboard.

Can I upgrade from Lite without downtime?

Yes. Upgrades apply in place and you pay only the prorated difference for the rest of the cycle.

Is a domain included?

Not in the price shown. Add one in the cart; .com is $13.65 for the first year and renews at the same rate.

Which payment methods work?

Cards, PayPal and bank transfer in AED or USD. Invoices are issued by Desert Tech Cloud Services LLC in Dubai.
