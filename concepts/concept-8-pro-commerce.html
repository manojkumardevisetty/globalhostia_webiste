Questions before you buy

What does "renews at" mean?

The first term is billed at the introductory price. After that, the plan renews at the regular price shown on the card. You can cancel before renewal from your dashboard.

Can I move an existing site?

Yes. Open a migration ticket after checkout and we'll move cPanel accounts, WordPress sites or plain files for free, usually within 24 hours.

Which location should I pick?

Pick the one closest to most of your visitors. Gulf audiences: UAE. North and South America: USA. South-East Asia and Australia: Singapore.

Can I upgrade later?

Any time. Upgrades are pro-rated and happen without downtime; the difference is added to your next invoice.
