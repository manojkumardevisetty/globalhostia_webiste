Questions people ask before buying

Can I move an existing site?

Yes, and it's free. After checkout, open a migration ticket with your old host's login. We move everything and confirm with you before DNS changes.

What does "unlimited websites" actually mean?

Add as many domains as you like to one cPanel account. Storage and CPU are shared by all of them, so very busy sites are better on a VPS.

Which server location should I pick?

Choose the one closest to most of your visitors. For GCC audiences pick UAE; for global audiences USA or Singapore work well. You can change later.

What happens at renewal?

Your plan renews at the regular price shown under each card. We email you 30, 14 and 3 days before, and you can cancel from the dashboard at any time.
