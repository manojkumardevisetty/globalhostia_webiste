    How can I order a new hosting plan?

    Ordering is simple! Browse our hosting plans, select the one that fits your needs, and click "Get Started Now". You'll be guided through our secure checkout process where you can configure your plan, register or transfer a domain, and complete payment.

    After payment confirmation, your hosting account will be provisioned within 5-15 minutes. You'll receive login credentials and setup instructions via email.

    What is reseller hosting?

    Reseller hosting allows you to create and sell your own hosting packages under your brand. You get a master cPanel account with WHM (Web Host Manager) control panel, allowing you to allocate resources, create custom packages, and manage multiple client accounts.

    Perfect for web designers, developers, or entrepreneurs wanting to start their own hosting business without infrastructure investment. We handle the hardware, network, and technical support while you focus on growing your client base.

    How do I register a new domain name?

    Use our domain search tool on the homepage to check availability. Enter your desired domain name (e.g., "yourbusiness.com"), and we'll show you available extensions with pricing. Popular TLDs include .com ($13.99), .net ($11.29), .org ($8.99), and many more.

    Domains can be registered standalone or bundled with hosting plans. All domains include free WHOIS privacy protection, DNS management, and email forwarding. Domain transfers from other registrars are also supported with competitive pricing.

    What payment methods do you accept?

    We accept all major payment methods for your convenience: Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, JCB, Maestro, Discover), PayPal for secure online payments, and Cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT) for those preferring digital currency transactions.

    All payments are processed through secure, PCI-DSS compliant gateways. Recurring subscriptions can be set up with cards or PayPal for automatic renewal, ensuring uninterrupted service.

    Is your hosting secure?

    Absolutely! Security is our top priority. All plans include free SSL certificates, automated daily backups with 30-day retention, advanced DDoS protection, malware scanning, and server-level firewalls. Our datacenters feature 24/7 security monitoring, biometric access controls, and redundant power systems.

    We also provide automatic software updates, isolated account architecture to prevent cross-contamination, and compliance with industry standards including ISO 27001 and SOC 2. Your data is safe with us.

    What's the difference between VPS and Dedicated Servers?

    VPS (Virtual Private Server) provides isolated resources on a shared physical server, starting at $8/month. You get guaranteed CPU, RAM, and storage with root access and full control. Ideal for growing websites, development environments, or applications needing dedicated resources without full server costs.

    Dedicated Servers ($39.99+/month) give you an entire physical server exclusively for your use. You get maximum performance, complete hardware control, and can customize everything from OS to security configurations. Perfect for high-traffic sites, resource-intensive applications, or businesses requiring complete isolation and maximum power.

    Do you offer money-back guarantee?

    Yes! We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all shared hosting and WordPress hosting plans. If you're not completely satisfied within the first 30 days, contact our support team for a full refund—no questions asked.

    VPS and Dedicated Server plans include a 7-day money-back guarantee due to resource allocation commitments. Domain registrations and SSL certificates are non-refundable as per industry standards, but we'll work with you to ensure complete satisfaction with your hosting experience.

    Can I upgrade my hosting plan later?

    Absolutely! You can upgrade anytime from your client portal. Moving from shared to VPS, VPS to dedicated, or increasing resources within the same tier is seamless. We'll migrate your data at no extra charge, and you only pay the prorated difference for the remaining billing cycle.

    Upgrades typically complete within 2-4 hours with zero downtime. Our migration team handles all technical aspects including DNS updates, file transfers, and database migrations. Downgrading is also possible, though subject to resource availability verification.

    What control panel do you provide?

    We provide cPanel/WHM, the industry's most popular and user-friendly control panel. It includes one-click app installer (Softaculous) with 400+ applications, email management, file manager, database tools (phpMyAdmin), FTP accounts, backup management, and comprehensive statistics.

    VPS and Dedicated customers can choose alternative panels like DirectAdmin, Plesk, or custom configurations. All control panels are updated regularly and include SSL management, security tools, and mobile-responsive interfaces for management on the go.

    Do you provide website migration services?

    Yes! We offer FREE website migration for all hosting plans. Our migration specialists will transfer your website, emails, databases, and files from your current host with zero downtime. Simply provide your old hosting credentials, and we handle everything.

    Migrations typically complete within 24-48 hours. We perform thorough testing before switching DNS to ensure everything works perfectly. For complex sites or enterprise migrations, we offer white-glove migration services with dedicated project managers and guaranteed SLAs.

    What is GDPR?

    The GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) is the most important development of Data Protection Law for decades. It will strengthen and ultimately replace the existing Data Protection Act (1998) and is designed to protect the personal data and privacy of citizens across Europe. GDPR will not be affected by the UK’s exit from the EU and the Regulation comes fully into effect on 25 May 2018.
