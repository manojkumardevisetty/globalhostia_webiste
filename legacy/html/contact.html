    • How can I order a new hosting plan?

      Ordering is simple! Browse our hosting plans, select the one that fits your needs, and click "Get Started Now". You'll be guided through our secure checkout process where you can configure your plan, register or transfer a domain, and complete payment.

      After payment confirmation, your hosting account will be provisioned within 5-15 minutes. You'll receive login credentials and setup instructions via email.

    • What is reseller hosting?

      Reseller hosting allows you to create and sell your own hosting packages under your brand. You get a master cPanel account with WHM (Web Host Manager) control panel, allowing you to allocate resources, create custom packages, and manage multiple client accounts.

      Perfect for web designers, developers, or entrepreneurs wanting to start their own hosting business without infrastructure investment. We handle the hardware, network, and technical support.

    • How do I register a new domain name?

      Use our domain search tool on the homepage to check availability. Enter your desired domain name (e.g., "yourbusiness.com"), and we'll show you available extensions with pricing. Popular TLDs include .com ($13.99), .net ($11.29), and .org ($8.99).

      Domains can be registered standalone or bundled with hosting plans. All domains include free WHOIS privacy protection, DNS management, and email forwarding.

    • What payment methods do you accept?

      We accept all major payment methods: Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, JCB, Maestro, Discover), PayPal for secure online payments, and Cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT) for digital currency transactions.

      All payments are processed through secure, PCI-DSS compliant gateways. Recurring subscriptions can be set up with cards or PayPal for automatic renewal.

    • Is your hosting secure?

      Absolutely! Security is our top priority. All plans include free SSL certificates, automated daily backups with 30-day retention, advanced DDoS protection, malware scanning, and server-level firewalls. Our datacenters feature 24/7 security monitoring and biometric access controls.

      We also provide automatic software updates, isolated account architecture, and compliance with ISO 27001 and SOC 2 standards.

      Yes! Our Managed WordPress hosting starting at $12.99/month includes one-click installation, pre-configured optimization, automatic updates, and WordPress-specific caching. Your site will be online in minutes with zero technical knowledge required.

    • What's the difference between VPS and Dedicated Servers?

      VPS (Virtual Private Server) provides isolated resources on a shared physical server, starting at $8/month. You get guaranteed CPU, RAM, and storage with root access. Ideal for growing websites or applications needing dedicated resources.

      Dedicated Servers ($39.99+/month) give you an entire physical server exclusively. You get maximum performance, complete hardware control, and full customization from OS to security. Perfect for high-traffic sites or resource-intensive applications.
